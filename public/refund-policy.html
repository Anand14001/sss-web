Refund Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

The entry fee

The entry fee for Salem Star Speaker is ₹999 per participant, the same for Junior, Teens and Adults. It covers one entry to the contest held on Sunday, 1 November 2026 at Hotel A Square Rivera, Salem.

No payment is taken on this website

This website does not collect payments. It has no payment gateway and no checkout. The fee is arranged directly with the organisers once they confirm your place, so any refund is handled by them through the same route.

Refund terms are set by the organisers and have not been published yet. Whether the entry fee can be refunded, by when, and in what circumstances — a withdrawal, a missed event, or a change to the contest date — has not been confirmed. Nothing here should be taken as a promise either way.

Please agree the refund terms with the organisers before you pay. Call Vivek on 86107 11306 or Tharu on 7010713565 and ask them to confirm the position in writing.

If the event is changed or cancelled

If the organisers change the date or cancel the contest, they will contact registered participants directly on the number given at registration, and will explain what happens to fees already paid.
