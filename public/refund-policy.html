Refund Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

The entry fee

The entry fee for Salem Star Speaker depends on the age category:

  • Juniors (10–13) — ₹99 per participant
  • Teens (14–17) — ₹199 per participant
  • Adults (18+) — ₹299 per participant

It covers one entry to the contest held on Sunday, 1 November 2026 at Hotel A Square Rivera, Salem.

How payment is taken

The entry fee is paid on this website at the time of registration, through Razorpay. Your place is confirmed only once the payment is verified, and you will receive a registration reference and a confirmation email at that point. Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by Razorpay and are never stored on our servers.

Refund terms have not been published yet. Whether the entry fee can be refunded, by when, and in what circumstances — a withdrawal, a missed event, or a change to the contest date — has not been confirmed by the organisers. Nothing here should be taken as a promise either way.

Please confirm the refund position with the organisers before you pay. Call Vivek on 86107 11306 or Tharu on 7010713565.

If the event is changed or cancelled

If the organisers change the date or cancel the contest, they will contact registered participants directly on the email address and number given at registration, and will explain what happens to fees already paid. Any refund that is agreed is returned to the account the payment was made from, through Razorpay.
