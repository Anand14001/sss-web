Salem Star Speaker

Terms & Conditions

Last updated 14 October 2026.

The event

Salem Star Speaker — The Speaking Contest is organised by Salem Parenting Club in association with Speak Up Salem Toastmasters Club. It takes place on Sunday, 1 November 2026 at Hotel A Square Rivera, Salem.

Who may enter

Entry is open from age 10, in three categories, judged on age as at 1 November 2026:

  • Junior — 10 to 13 years
  • Teens — 14 to 17 years
  • Adults — 18 years and above

Participants must enter the category matching their age on the contest date. Entrants under 18 should be registered by a parent or guardian.

Registration and fee

  • The entry fee is ₹99 for Juniors (10–13), ₹199 for Teens (14–17) and ₹299 for Adults (18+), per participant.
  • The fee is paid on this website through Razorpay when you register. A place is confirmed only once the payment has been verified.
  • Registration closes on 26 October 2026.
  • Places are limited. The organisers may close registration earlier if all places are taken.
  • A place is confirmed by the organisers. Completing the form on this website is not by itself a registration.

Format

Preliminary rounds may be conducted if there are more participants than the final round can accommodate. The top three winners in each category receive prizes. Prize details are announced by the organisers closer to the event.

Changes to the event

The organisers may change the schedule, format or arrangements where circumstances require it, and will tell registered participants directly.

Still to be published. The detailed contest rules — speech topics and time limits, the running order, judging criteria, conduct requirements and grounds for disqualification — have not been issued yet. They are set by the organisers, and nothing on this website should be read as stating them.
