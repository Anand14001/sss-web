Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

What this website collects

When you complete the registration form and pay, the details you enter are sent to our server and stored so that the organisers can run the event:

  • the participant's full name;
  • an email address and a phone number, so we can contact you;
  • the date of birth and age category, to confirm the right category;
  • occupation and city or town;
  • a parent or guardian's name and phone number, where the participant is under 18 and these are given.

We also store your registration reference, the amount due and the status of your payment. The site runs no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sets no cookies for those purposes.

Until you submit the form, what you type stays in your own browser. Closing or refreshing the page before submitting discards it.

Payment details

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Your card, UPI or bank details are entered on Razorpay's own secure checkout and go directly to them — they are never sent to, seen by, or stored on our servers. We receive only a payment reference, the amount and whether the payment succeeded. Razorpay handles that data under its own privacy policy at razorpay.com/privacy.

To create your payment, we pass Razorpay your name, email address and phone number so it can issue a receipt and contact you about the transaction.

How it is used

These details are used only to organise Salem Star Speaker and to contact you about it — confirming your place, sharing the reporting time and venue details, and announcing results. They are not sold, and they are not used for unrelated marketing.

Participants under 18

The contest is open from age 10. Where a participant is under 18, a parent or guardian should register on their behalf and is the person the organisers will contact.
