Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

What this website collects

Nothing. This website has no database, sends no data to any server, and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. It sets no cookies.

Anything you type into the registration form stays in your own browser. Pressing Register Now only checks that your answers are complete and consistent — it does not transmit them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page discards what you entered.

What the organisers collect

Registration for the contest is handled directly by the organisers, by phone. When you register with them they will record the details needed to run the event:

  • the participant's full name;
  • an email address and a phone number, so they can contact you;
  • the age category and date of birth, to confirm the right category;
  • occupation and city or town.

How it is used

These details are used only to organise Salem Star Speaker and to contact you about it — confirming your place, sharing the reporting time and venue details, and announcing results. They are not sold, and they are not used for unrelated marketing.

Participants under 18

The contest is open from age 10. Where a participant is under 18, a parent or guardian should register on their behalf and is the person the organisers will contact.

Third parties
